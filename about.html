About Us

Dedicated to nurturing holistic health through Yoga, Reiki Healing, Meditation and Natural Wellness.

Welcome to Manasvini : The Sattva

Manasvini : The Sattva is a holistic Yoga, Reiki Healing and Wellness Centre situated in Ajmer, Rajasthan. Our vision is to empower individuals to achieve physical vitality, emotional balance, mental clarity and spiritual growth through authentic yogic practices and energy healing therapies. We believe that every person possesses the ability to heal from within. Through personalized guidance and holistic wellness programs, we help people restore harmony in body, mind and soul.

Mr. Vishwas Pareek is a renowned Yoga Therapist based in Ajmer, dedicated to promoting holistic health through traditional yoga, therapeutic practices and natural healing techniques.
